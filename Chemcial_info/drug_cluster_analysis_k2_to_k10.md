# Drug Clustering Narrative: From 2 to 10 Clusters

## Overview

We have a dataset of **380 drugs**, each represented as a **768-dimensional embedding vector** generated by the **MolFormer** transformer model from its SMILES string. Embeddings capture the *chemical character* of each molecule — its size, shape, polarity, aromaticity, and functional groups — in a form the model can reason about.

We applied **K-Means clustering** (`scikit-learn`, `random_state=42`, 20 restarts) to these 380 vectors, asking the algorithm to partition the drugs into **k groups**. We then walked k from **2 up to 10**, and for each value examined **what kind of chemicals landed together in each cluster**, using:

- **RDKit chemical descriptors** (median per cluster): molecular weight (MW), cLogP (lipophilicity), TPSA (polar surface area), H-bond donors/acceptors, ring counts, rotatable bonds
- **Drug metadata** (mechanism of action, MoA) from `drug_metadata.parquet`

The story that emerges is a **hierarchical unfolding**: the dataset first splits into a small number of very broad chemical families, and as k grows, these families split into more and more specific chemical sub-types. Below is the full narrative for every clustering from k = 2 to k = 10.

## k = 2 — The Two Grand Families

At the coarsest level, the algorithm sees **two dominant chemical civilizations** in the drug space.

### Cluster 0 — The Polar, Hydrogen-Bond-Rich World *(n = 128, 33.7%)*

This is the cluster of **hydrophilic, polar, hydrogen-bond-heavy molecules**. The median drug here has a **low logP (≈1.5)**, a **large polar surface area (104 Å²)**, and more H-bond donors and acceptors (3 / 6) than its sibling cluster. Molecules tend to be **smaller (MW ≈ 385)**, **rigid (only ~4 rotatable bonds)**, and carry **few aromatic rings (1)**.

It is a chemical grab-bag of the "wet" corner of drug space:

- **Nucleoside and nucleotide analogs** — 5-Fluorouracil, Gemcitabine, Cytarabine, Decitabine, Azacitidine
- **Small polar phenols and polyphenols** — Gallic acid, Resveratrol, Salicylic acid, Ferulic acid, Arbutin
- **Small polar monomers and salts** — DMSO (the solvent), Talc, Cysteamine, Hydroxyurea, Eflornithine
- **Catecholamines and polar hormones** — Norepinephrine, L-Thyroxine
- **Tetracyclines** — Tetracycline, Doxycycline, Demeclocycline

Biologically, it is enriched for **DNA synthesis / repair inhibitors** (13 drugs), reflecting the nucleoside content. These are the small, water-soluble, bioavailable molecules — often old drugs, antimetabolites, or nutraceutical-like structures.

### Cluster 1 — The Lipophilic, Aromatic Drug World *(n = 252, 66.3%)*

This is the cluster of **classic small-molecule drug scaffolds**: **lipophilic (logP ≈ 3.4)**, **aromatic-rich (median 3 aromatic rings)**, and **drug-like in every dimension** — MW ≈ 434, 2 H-bond donors, 6 acceptors, 5 rotatable bonds.

Virtually the entire **modern medicinal-chemistry space** lives here:

- **Kinase inhibitors of every flavor** — ERK5-IN-2, Pexidartinib, Larotrectinib, Trametinib, Erdafitinib, and dozens more
- **HIV protease inhibitors** — Saquinavir, Ritonavir, Lopinavir
- **Antifolates and antimetabolites** — Methotrexate, Pemetrexed, Folic acid
- **Azoles and other heterocyclic drugs** — Econazole, Clotrimazole, Voriconazole
- **Classic small-molecule drugs** — Bortezomib, Carbamazepine, Triclosan

Biologically, it is dominated by **kinase and enzyme inhibitors** (other TK inhibitors ×14, EGFR/ERBB inhibitors ×12, MAPK inhibitors ×11). These are the flat, nitrogen-rich, moderately lipophilic molecules that medicinal chemists make by the thousands.

> **The big picture at k=2:** the entire drug collection splits into *polar, small, H-bond-rich molecules* (nucleosides, phenols, salts, polar hormones) versus *lipophilic, aromatic, drug-like scaffolds* (kinase inhibitors, heterocycles, modern drugs).

## k = 3 — The Polar World Splits into Two

At k=3, the aromatic drug world (previous Cluster 1) remains essentially intact, while the **polar cluster splits into two distinct families**.

### Cluster 0 — Small Polar Molecules: Nucleosides, Phenols & Polar Hormones *(n = 100, 26.3%)*

This branch keeps the **smallest and most polar molecules**: MW ≈ 393, logP ≈ 2.1, TPSA ≈ 105, with few rotatable bonds (≈3) and few aromatic rings (≈1). The archetypal members are:

- **Nucleoside analogs** — 5-Fluorouracil, Gemcitabine, Cytarabine, Adenosine
- **Polyphenols and phenolic acids** — Gallic acid, Resveratrol, Salicylic acid, Daidzin, Arbutin
- **Catecholamines** — Norepinephrine, Vilanterol, Phenylephrine
- **Small polar heterocycles** — DMSO, Triclosan, 8-Hydroxyquinoline
- **Tetracyclines and polar antibiotics** — Tetracycline, Doxycycline

Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors** (10). These are the small, water-friendly building-block-like molecules.

### Cluster 1 — Flexible, Polar "Linker" Molecules & Peptidomimetics *(n = 93, 24.5%)*

This branch keeps molecules that are **still polar but more flexible and chain-like**: MW ≈ 331, logP ≈ 2.0, but with **5 rotatable bonds** and fewer rings (≈2). The signature members:

- **Boron-containing proteasome inhibitors** — Bortezomib, Ixazomib
- **Peptides and peptidomimetics** — Pentagastrin, Bestatin, Darinaparsin
- **Flexible polar chains** — Lipoic acid, Fumaric acid, Hydroxyurea, Eflornithine
- **Polar sulfonamides/ureas** — Furosemide, Acetazolamide, Pentamidine
- **Inorganic salts** — Calcium lactate, Sodium salicylate, L-Thyroxine sodium

Biologically it is enriched for **cyclooxygenase (COX) inhibitors** (5) and proteasome inhibitors — a medley of molecules connected by *flexibility* rather than by a single scaffold.

### Cluster 2 — The Lipophilic Aromatic Drug World *(n = 187, 49.2%)*

Nearly identical in character to the k=2 aromatic cluster: **logP ≈ 3.8, 3 aromatic rings, MW ≈ 454, 2 HBD / 6 HBA**. This is the same large family of kinase inhibitors and heterocyclic drugs (ERK5-IN-2, Pexidartinib, Pemetrexed, Econazole, Tucidinostat, etc.), enriched for **TK inhibitors (13), EGFR/ERBB (12), multi-TK (10), MAPK (10)**.

> **The big picture at k=3:** *aromatic drug scaffolds* (49%) | *small polar monomers* (26%) | *flexible polar chains and peptidomimetics* (25%).

## k = 4 — The First Natural-Product Branch Emerges

At k=4, a **new and very interesting cluster appears**: a family of **large, rigid, natural-product-like molecules** (steroids, taxanes, and complex polycyclics) splits off from the aromatic drug world.

### Cluster 0 — Steroids, Retinoids & Rigid Natural-Product Scaffolds *(n = 61, 16.1%)*

The signature here is **large but rigid**: MW ≈ 505, logP ≈ 3.1, **TPSA ≈ 124 Å²**, **5 rings but only 1 aromatic**, and only **6 rotatable bonds**. These are the fused-ring, polycyclic molecules:

- **Steroids and steroid-like drugs** — Abiraterone acetate, Mifepristone, Megestrol, Finasteride, Fulvestrant
- **Taxanes** — Paclitaxel, Docetaxel
- **Retinoids** — Retinoic acid, Peretinoin
- **Flavonoids and glycosides** — Daidzin, Glycyrrhizic acid
- **Alkaloid natural products** — Harringtonine, Homoharringtonine
- **Renin inhibitors** — Aliskiren

Enriched for **microtubule inhibitors (5)** and glucocorticoid receptor agonists (4). These are the *big, shape-constrained, natural-product-derived* drugs — often anti-cancer compounds isolated from plants.

### Cluster 1 — Small Aromatic "Classical" Drugs *(n = 88, 23.2%)*

A compact cluster of **small, moderately lipophilic, aromatic drugs**: MW ≈ 330, logP ≈ 2.2, **2 aromatic rings**, 5 rotatable bonds. These are the familiar workhorse drugs:

- **Bortezomib** (proteasome inhibitor), **Carbamazepine**, **Belinostat**, **Riluzole**, **Minodronic acid**
- **Pentamidine**, **Furosemide**, **Balsalazide**, **Pemetrexed**

A mixed but coherent group of *modest-size aromatic small molecules*.

### Cluster 2 — The Lipophilic Aromatic Drug World *(n = 181, 47.6%)*

The large kinase-inhibitor family persists (MW ≈ 454, logP ≈ 3.9, 3 aromatic rings), still enriched for **TK inhibitors (13), EGFR/ERBB (12), multi-TK (10)**. Unchanged in character.

### Cluster 3 — Small, Hydrophilic Molecules & Salts *(n = 50, 13.2%)*

The **smallest, most polar** group: MW ≈ 244, logP ≈ 0.1, TPSA ≈ 98, only ~2 rotatable bonds. Members:

- **Nucleoside analogs** — 5-Fluorouracil, Hydroxyurea, Allantoin
- **Small polar molecules** — DMSO, Cysteamine, Gallic acid
- **Salts and inorganics** — Talc, Calcium lactate, L-Thyroxine sodium
- **Ixazomib** (boron-based proteasome inhibitor)

Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors (9)** and COX inhibitors (4).

> **The big picture at k=4:** the aromatic world splits into *classical small aromatics* (23%) and *big lipophilic scaffolds* (48%); the natural-product branch (steroids/taxanes, 16%) and the small polar group (13%) become their own islands.

## k = 5 — The Flexible-Chain Cluster Is Born

At k=5, the *flexible polar linker* molecules finally get their own cluster, and the natural-product family matures.

### Cluster 0 — Medium Aromatic Drugs: Folates, NSAIDs & Anticonvulsants *(n = 98, 25.8%)*

A middle-of-the-road aromatic cluster: MW ≈ 347, logP ≈ 2.8, **2 aromatic rings**, 5 rotatable bonds. Members span:

- **Antifolates / antimetabolites** — Folic acid, Pemetrexed, Entecavir
- **NSAIDs** — Oxaprozin (COX inhibitors ×7 enrich this cluster)
- **Anticonvulsants** — Phenytoin, Carbamazepine
- **HDAC inhibitors** — Tucidinostat, Belinostat
- **Proteasome inhibitor** — Bortezomib

A cluster of *well-behaved, medium-size aromatic drugs* — the "typical small-molecule drug" in the middle of the MW range.

### Cluster 1 — The Lipophilic Kinase-Inhibitor Army *(n = 148, 38.9%)*

The biggest cluster remains the **lipophilic kinase inhibitor family**: MW ≈ 475, logP ≈ 4.2, **3 aromatic rings**, 2 HBD / 6 HBA. Enriched heavily for **other TK inhibitors (13), MAPK (10), EGFR/ERBB (10), multi-TK (9)**. These are the "flat, greasy, heteroaromatic" kinase scaffolds — ERK5-IN-2, Pexidartinib, Larotrectinib, GSK1059615, and many more.

### Cluster 2 — Flexible Polar Chains & Peptidomimetics *(n = 31, 8.2%)*

The **most flexible cluster in the dataset**: MW ≈ 411 but **9 rotatable bonds**, only ~1 ring, logP ≈ 1.0, TPSA ≈ 113. The members are chain-like and floppy:

- **Peptidomimetics** — Ixazomib citrate, DTP3, Pentagastrin, Darinaparsin
- **Flexible acids & chains** — Lipoic acid, Fumaric acid, Sivelestat
- **β-blockers** — Bisoprolol
- **Salts** — Calcium lactate
- **Protease inhibitors** — Atazanavir

### Cluster 3 — Small Hydrophilic Molecules & Nucleosides *(n = 47, 12.4%)*

Same as before: MW ≈ 244, logP ≈ 0.1, TPSA ≈ 99, ~2 rotatable bonds. DMSO, Talc, Hydroxyurea, Cysteamine, 5-Fluorouracil, Gallic acid, and friends. Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors (9)**.

### Cluster 4 — Complex Polycyclic Natural Products *(n = 56, 14.7%)*

The natural-product family matures into a **large, non-aromatic, polycyclic** cluster: MW ≈ 511, logP ≈ 3.0, TPSA ≈ 124, **5 rings, 0 aromatic**, 6 rotatable bonds. Members:

- **Taxanes** — Paclitaxel, Docetaxel
- **Steroids** — Abiraterone, Mifepristone, Fulvestrant
- **Cephalotaxus alkaloids** — Harringtonine, Homoharringtonine
- **Saponins/glycosides** — Glycyrrhizic acid, Daidzin
- **Retinoids** — Retinoic acid, Peretinoin

Enriched for **microtubule inhibitors (5)** — a beautiful, coherent anti-mitotic natural-product cluster.

> **The big picture at k=5:** *kinase army* (39%) | *medium aromatic drugs* (26%) | *polycyclic natural products* (15%) | *small polar* (12%) | *flexible chains* (8%).

## k = 6 — The Kinase Army Begins to Split

At k=6, the large lipophilic aromatic cluster finally starts to divide, while a tiny but very distinctive **ultra-polar cluster** appears.

### Cluster 0 — Small Hydrophilic Molecules & Salts *(n = 44, 11.6%)*

MW ≈ 228, logP ≈ 0.0, TPSA ≈ 95, ~2 rotatable bonds. The small polar group: DMSO, Talc, Ixazomib, Hydroxyurea, Cysteamine, 5-Fluorouracil, Gallic acid, L-Thyroxine. Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors (8)**.

### Cluster 1 — Medium Aromatic Drugs (Mixed Therapeutics) *(n = 95, 25.0%)*

MW ≈ 362, logP ≈ 3.0, TPSA ≈ 83, **3 aromatic rings**, 4 rotatable bonds. A coherent mid-size aromatic cluster holding Entecavir, Folic acid, Pemetrexed, Clotrimazole, Phenytoin, Oxaprozin, Belinostat, Carbamazepine, Riluzole. Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors (9)** and **COX inhibitors (6)**.

### Cluster 2 — Large Polycyclic Natural Products *(n = 54, 14.2%)*

MW ≈ 524, logP ≈ 3.0, TPSA ≈ 126, **5 rings, 0 aromatic**. Steroids, taxanes, glycosides (Abiraterone, Paclitaxel, Docetaxel, Glycyrrhizic acid, Daidzin, Harringtonine). Enriched for **microtubule inhibitors (5)**.

### Cluster 3 — The Ultra-Polar H-Bond-Rich Specialists *(n = 10, 2.6%)*

A **tiny but extremely distinctive** cluster: MW ≈ 411, **logP ≈ 0.1**, **TPSA ≈ 131**, **4 HBD / 8 HBA**, only 2 rotatable bonds. These are the most hydrogen-bond-dense drug molecules in the dataset:

- **Tetracyclines** — Tetracycline, Doxycycline, Demeclocycline
- **Nucleoside analogs** — 5-Azacytidine, Clofarabine
- **Polar steroids** — Dexamethasone, Budesonide
- **Flavone glycoside** — Baicalin

Enriched for **glucocorticoid receptor agonists (2)**. This is the "tetracycline + polar steroid" micro-world.

### Cluster 4 — Flexible Polar Chains & Peptidomimetics *(n = 53, 13.9%)*

MW ≈ 332, logP ≈ 1.5, TPSA ≈ 87, ~1 ring, **7 rotatable bonds**. Bortezomib, Ixazomib citrate, Bisoprolol, Fumaric acid, Darinaparsin, Pentamidine, Lipoic acid, Acetazolamide, DTP3 — the flexible-chain family, now bigger.

### Cluster 5 — The Lipophilic Kinase-Inhibitor Army (Split-off) *(n = 124, 32.6%)*

The greasiest corner of kinase space: MW ≈ 501, **logP ≈ 4.5**, 3 aromatic rings. Mitoxantrone, PF-06260933, Econazole, Pexidartinib, Larotrectinib, olaparib, and ~120 more. Enriched for **other TK inhibitors (12), EGFR/ERBB (10), MAPK (8)**.

> **The big picture at k=6:** the kinase army (33%) separates from mid-size aromatics (25%); natural products (14%), flexible chains (14%), small polar (12%), and the tiny ultra-polar specialist cluster (3%) fill out the map.

## k = 7 — The Ultra-Polar Glycoside Cluster Emerges

At k=7, a beautiful new cluster appears: the **very polar, polyhydroxylated glycoside-like molecules** separate from the natural products.

### Cluster 0 — Drug-Like Kinase Scaffolds (Heteroaromatic) *(n = 121, 31.8%)*

MW ≈ 462, logP ≈ 3.9, TPSA ≈ 98, **3 aromatic rings**, 2 HBD / 7 HBA. The main kinase family: Entecavir, Pemetrexed, Pexidartinib, Larotrectinib, olaparib, etc. Enriched for **other TK inhibitors (12), EGFR/ERBB (8)**.

### Cluster 1 — Small Aromatic Classical Drugs *(n = 71, 18.7%)*

MW ≈ 322, logP ≈ 2.8, **2 aromatic rings**, 4 rotatable bonds. Bortezomib, Pentamidine, Phenytoin, Belinostat, Carbamazepine, Riluzole, Allopurinol, Clonidine. Enriched for **COX inhibitors (7)**.

### Cluster 2 — Lipophilic Aromatics & Phenols (Mixed) *(n = 60, 15.8%)*

MW ≈ 449, **logP ≈ 4.1**, TPSA ≈ 78, 3 aromatic rings. A more lipophilic aromatic group: ERK5-IN-2, Vilanterol, Niclosamide, Triclosan, Mitoxantrone, Resveratrol, Econazole. Enriched for **MAPK (5), multi-TK (4), MEK (3)** — plus several phenols and lipophilic natural aromatics.

### Cluster 3 — Small Hydrophilic Molecules & Salts *(n = 34, 8.9%)*

MW ≈ 213, **logP ≈ −0.4**, TPSA ≈ 98, ~2 rotatable bonds. The smallest, most water-soluble group: DMSO, Talc, Hydroxyurea, Eflornithine, Cysteamine, Allantoin, 5-Fluorouracil, Gallic acid. Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors (8)**.

### Cluster 4 — Ultra-Polar Polyhydroxylated Glycosides & Conjugates *(n = 21, 5.5%)*

**New and fascinating**: MW ≈ 471, logP ≈ 1.8, **TPSA ≈ 183**, **5 HBD / 9 HBA**, ~0 aromatic rings. These are the *sugar-heavy, polyhydroxylated* molecules:

- **Flavone/rutin glycosides** — Baicalin, Rutin, Hesperidin
- **Tetracyclines** — Tetracycline, Doxycycline
- **Cardiac glycoside** — Digitoxin
- **Saponins** — Glycyrrhizic acid
- **Nucleoside analogs** — 5-Azacytidine, Clofarabine
- **Polar steroids** — Dexamethasone

### Cluster 5 — Steroids, Taxanes & Rigid Natural Products *(n = 45, 11.8%)*

MW ≈ 446, logP ≈ 2.9, TPSA ≈ 107, **5 rings (1 aromatic)**, 5 rotatable bonds. Abiraterone, Harringtonine, Mifepristone, Retinoic acid, Paclitaxel, Docetaxel, Megestrol, Triamcinolone. Enriched for **microtubule inhibitors (5)** and retinoid agonists (2).

### Cluster 6 — Flexible Polar Chains & Peptidomimetics *(n = 28, 7.4%)*

MW ≈ 438, logP ≈ 1.2, TPSA ≈ 118, **~1 ring, 10 rotatable bonds**. The most flexible cluster: Ixazomib citrate, Bisoprolol, Fumaric acid, Darinaparsin, Lipoic acid, DTP3, Sivelestat, Atazanavir, Pentagastrin, Thymopentin, Aliskiren, Goserelin.

> **The big picture at k=7:** the natural-product world splits into *polyhydroxylated glycosides* (5.5%) and *steroids/taxanes* (12%); the rest of the map stays recognizable.

## k = 8 — Small Phenols Get Their Own Cluster

At k=8, the **small phenolic/polyphenolic molecules** (flavonoids, salicylates, catechols) separate into their own well-defined cluster.

### Cluster 0 — Flexible Polar Chains & Peptidomimetics *(n = 38, 10.0%)*

MW ≈ 385, logP ≈ 1.5, TPSA ≈ 113, **~1 ring, 8 rotatable bonds**. Bortezomib, Ixazomib citrate, Bisoprolol, Fumaric acid, Darinaparsin, Pentamidine, Lipoic acid, DTP3, Aspirin, Sivelestat, Atazanavir, Pentagastrin — the flexible-chain family, now cleanly isolated.

### Cluster 1 — Lipophilic Aromatic Kinase Inhibitors *(n = 55, 14.5%)*

MW ≈ 477, **logP ≈ 4.4**, TPSA ≈ 78, 3 aromatic rings, 6 rotatable bonds. Mitoxantrone, PF-06260933, Econazole, Ataluren, 4EGI-1, Cobimetinib, Penfluridol, Bimiralisib, Terfenadine. Enriched for **MAPK (4), multi-TK (4), MEK (3)**.

### Cluster 2 — Large Polycyclic Natural Products & Macrocycles *(n = 48, 12.6%)*

MW ≈ 540, logP ≈ 2.9, **TPSA ≈ 133**, 5 rings, **0 aromatic**. Abiraterone, Harringtonine, Mifepristone, Glycyrrhizic acid, Paclitaxel, Docetaxel, Megestrol, Triamcinolone, Drospirenone. Enriched for **microtubule inhibitors (5)**.

### Cluster 3 — Small Phenols & Polyphenols *(n = 25, 6.6%)*

**New**: MW ≈ 226, logP ≈ 1.8, TPSA ≈ 67, 1 ring (1 aromatic), 3 rotatable bonds. The phenolic family:

- **Phenolic acids** — Gallic acid, Salicylic acid, Arbutin
- **Polyphenols** — Resveratrol, Daidzin
- **Catecholamines** — Norepinephrine, Vilanterol
- **Phenolic antimicrobials** — Triclosan, 8-Hydroxyquinoline

Enriched for **COX inhibitors (3)** and adrenoceptor agonists (3) — a coherent, natural-product-flavored phenolic cluster.

### Cluster 4 — Ultra-Polar H-Bond-Rich Specialists *(n = 11, 2.9%)*

MW ≈ 430, **logP ≈ 0.1, TPSA ≈ 144, 4 HBD / 9 HBA**, ~2 rotatable bonds. Tetracyclines, 5-Azacytidine, Clofarabine, Baicalin, Dexamethasone, Budesonide, Cyclosporin A, Belzutifan — the most hydrogen-bond-dense molecules in the dataset.

### Cluster 5 — Medium Aromatic Drugs *(n = 69, 18.2%)*

MW ≈ 322, logP ≈ 3.0, TPSA ≈ 75, 2 aromatic rings, 4 rotatable bonds. ERK5-IN-2, Clotrimazole, Phenytoin, Oxaprozin, Belinostat, Carbamazepine, Riluzole, Allopurinol. Enriched for **COX inhibitors (7)**.

### Cluster 6 — Small Hydrophilic Molecules & Nucleosides *(n = 30, 7.9%)*

MW ≈ 273, **logP ≈ −1.3**, TPSA ≈ 112, 2 rings, ~2 rotatable bonds. DMSO, Talc, Ixazomib, Hydroxyurea, Eflornithine, Cysteamine, Entecavir, Allantoin, 5-Fluorouracil, L-Thyroxine. Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors (9)** — the nucleoside-heavy cluster.

### Cluster 7 — Drug-Like Kinase Scaffolds (Heteroaromatic) *(n = 104, 27.4%)*

MW ≈ 472, logP ≈ 4.0, TPSA ≈ 104, **4 aromatic rings**, 2 HBD / 8 HBA. Folic acid, Pemetrexed, Pexidartinib, Larotrectinib, olaparib, and the bulk of kinase inhibitors. Enriched for **other TK inhibitors (10), EGFR/ERBB (8)**.

> **The big picture at k=8:** phenols get their own cluster (6.6%); everything else stays consistent — kinase scaffolds (27%), medium aromatics (18%), lipophilic kinases (14%), natural products (13%), flexible chains (10%), small polar (8%), ultra-polar (3%).

## k = 9 — The Full-Resolved Chemical Map

At k=9, the dataset reaches its **most interpretable resolution**: every cluster now corresponds to a clean, identifiable chemical archetype. This is the recommended working clustering.

### Cluster 0 — Ultra-Polar H-Bond-Rich Specialists *(n = 11, 2.9%)*

The most hydrogen-bond-dense molecules in the collection: **MW ≈ 392, logP ≈ 0.1, TPSA ≈ 119, 4 HBD / 8 HBA, ~2 rotatable bonds**. Rigid and extremely polar.

- **Tetracyclines** — Tetracycline, Doxycycline, Demeclocycline
- **Nucleoside analogs** — 5-Azacytidine, Clofarabine
- **Polar steroids** — Dexamethasone, Budesonide
- **Catecholamine** — Norepinephrine; **flavone** — Baicalin; **HIF-2α inhibitor** — Belzutifan

Enriched for **glucocorticoid receptor agonists**. These are the "tetracycline + polar steroid + nucleoside" specialists.

### Cluster 1 — Lipophilic Heteroaromatic Kinase Inhibitors (RAF/MEK Flavor) *(n = 52, 13.7%)*

**MW ≈ 453, logP ≈ 4.3, TPSA ≈ 78, 3 aromatic rings, 2 HBD / 6 HBA.** The greasier, nitrogen-rich kinase inhibitors:

- **RAF inhibitors** — Dabrafenib, Vemurafenib, Encorafenib (significantly enriched, padj = 0.02)
- **MEK/MAPK pathway inhibitors** — Cobimetinib, Binimetinib, Trametinib, TAK-733
- **TKIs** — Crizotinib, Afatinib, Gefitinib, Vandetanib, Regorafenib
- **Other lipophilic aromatics** — Verapamil, Rimonabant, Econazole, Niclosamide

### Cluster 2 — Small Polar Molecules: Nucleosides, Phenols & Monomers *(n = 35, 9.2%)*

**MW ≈ 243, logP ≈ 0.3, TPSA ≈ 91, 2 rings (1 aromatic), 3 HBD / 4 HBA, ~2 rotatable bonds.** The small water-soluble building blocks:

- **Nucleoside analogs** — 5-Fluorouracil, Gemcitabine, Cytarabine, Decitabine, Trifluridine, Brivudine
- **Polyphenols** — Gallic acid, Resveratrol, Ferulic acid, Salicylic acid
- **Small polar molecules** — DMSO, Cysteamine, Ixazomib, Triclosan, 8-Hydroxyquinoline, Pyridoxine

Enriched for **DNA synthesis/repair inhibitors (6)**.

### Cluster 3 — Very Hydrophilic Acyclic Molecules, Salts & Metal Complexes *(n = 13, 3.4%)*

**MW ≈ 397, logP ≈ −2.3 (the most hydrophilic), TPSA ≈ 122, ~1 ring, 0 aromatic, 5 rotatable bonds.** Linear, charged, salt-like chemistry:

- **Metal complexes** — Oxaliplatin (platinum), Auranofin (gold)
- **Salts & inorganics** — Talc, Calcium lactate, Sodium salicylate
- **Linear charged molecules** — Hydroxyurea, Eflornithine, Busulfan, S-Adenosyl-L-methionine
- **Polar drug salts** — Balsalazide sodium, Sivelestat sodium, Fostamatinib disodium, L-Thyroxine sodium

### Cluster 4 — The Classic Kinase-Inhibitor Scaffold *(n = 81, 21.3%)*

The **largest and most representative cluster**: **MW ≈ 460, logP ≈ 4.2, TPSA ≈ 93, 4–5 rings with ~4 aromatic, 2 HBD / 7 HBA** — the textbook ATP-pocket-binding profile.

Significantly enriched for **other TK inhibitors (11, padj = 7e-4), GSK3 inhibitors (4), RAS inhibitors (4)**:

- **TKIs** — Larotrectinib, Erdafitinib, Ponatinib, Pralsetinib, Capmatinib, Neratinib, Tucatinib, Tirabrutinib
- **CDK inhibitors** — Palbociclib, Ribociclib
- **MEK/RAS** — Trametinib, Adagrasib
- **Others** — Venetoclax, olaparib, Sapanisertib, Filgotinib, Posaconazole

### Cluster 5 — Modern Oral Small-Molecule Drugs *(n = 71, 18.7%)*

**MW ≈ 421, logP ≈ 3.4, TPSA ≈ 98, 3 aromatic rings, 5 rotatable bonds.** The "typical drug cabinet" — well-behaved oral drugs of moderate size:

- **Antifolates** — Methotrexate, Pemetrexed, Folic acid, Raltitrexed
- **HIV protease inhibitors** — Saquinavir, Ritonavir, Lopinavir
- **NSAIDs/COX inhibitors** — Oxaprozin, Celecoxib, Valdecoxib, Piroxicam
- **Kinase drugs** — Sunitinib, Erlotinib, Osimertinib, Tofacitinib
- **Antivirals / misc.** — Entecavir, Selinexor, Mebendazole, Rosiglitazone, Omeprazole

### Cluster 6 — Flexible Polar Chains & Peptidomimetics *(n = 24, 6.3%)*

**MW ≈ 423, logP ≈ 1.5, TPSA ≈ 117, ~1 ring, 11 rotatable bonds (most flexible).** Chain-like, floppy molecules:

- **Peptides** — Pentagastrin, Thymopentin, DTP3, Daptomycin
- **ACE inhibitors** — Captopril, Perindopril
- **β-blockers** — Bisoprolol, Esmolol
- **Protease/other** — Atazanavir, Aliskiren, Bestatin
- **Simple chains** — Lipoic acid, Oleic acid, Fumaric acid, Orlistat, Dimethyl fumarate

### Cluster 7 — Small Aromatic "Classical" Drugs *(n = 43, 11.3%)*

## k = 10 — The Maximum Sensible Resolution

At k=10 — the **maximum number of clusters that still yields meaningful, chemically coherent groups** — the large natural-product cluster finally splits, and two small specialist clusters reach their final clean form.

### Cluster 0 — Drug-Like Kinase Scaffolds (TK/EGFR Flavor) *(n = 89, 23.4%)*

**MW ≈ 429, logP ≈ 3.5, TPSA ≈ 88, 3 aromatic rings, 2 HBD / 6 HBA.** The main body of kinase inhibitors:

- Enriched for **other TK inhibitors (9), EGFR/ERBB (7), MAPK (6), CDK (4)**
